# Latch Scan privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

Latch Scan is a document scanner and PDF toolkit that works entirely on your phone.

**What the app accesses**

- **Camera**: used only while the scanner screen is open, to find page edges and take photos of documents.
- **Photos and files you choose**: only the specific images, PDFs, Word or text files you pick with the system picker, so they can be scanned, merged or compressed.

**What the app collects or shares**

Nothing. Latch Scan has no internet permission, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your scans and files never leave your device unless you choose to share or save them yourself (for example with the Share button).

**Storage**

Saved scans are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Files you export with "Save" go to your Downloads folder (or the location you choose).

**Children**

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
